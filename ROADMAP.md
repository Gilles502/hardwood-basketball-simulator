# Roadmap

What is coming to Hardwood Basketball Simulator. Plans can change: the order follows what players run into most.
Ideas and bug reports are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/Gilles502/hardwood-basketball-simulator/issues).

## Next update: rookies and player development

- Every draft prospect gets a profile (a "story"): the raw teenager, the ready college senior, the European pro,
  and more. It decides where he starts and how he can grow.
- Clearer growth: you can see when and why a young player improves, and what playing time does for him.
- Veterans who mentor young teammates.
- A development view that shows each young player's outlook.

## After that

- **In-season cup**: a mid-season tournament with group games that also count in the standings.
- **End-of-game basketball**: timeouts, last shots, two-for-one, fouling when behind.
- **Shot chart**: every shot placed on the court, for games, players and teams.
- **Impact metrics**: BPM, VORP and Win Shares.
- **Smarter front offices**: GMs who remember past trades, more kinds of trade terms.
- **In-game editor** for draft classes and rosters, to build your own leagues.

## Further out

- Deeper scouting, a draft combine, players who go abroad and come back.
- Team chemistry and locker-room leaders.
- Press conferences, retired-jersey and Hall of Fame ceremonies.
- Sharing mods.
