# Changelog

## v0.1.1 Early Access

- Fixed: the game crashed at start when the zip was downloaded from GitHub and extracted with Explorer
  (Windows blocked a file the game window needs). The game now unblocks its own files; if it still cannot,
  it explains how to unblock the zip.

## v0.1.0 Early Access

First public release.

- A fictional 30-team league starting in 2002. Real-name leagues can be added as mods.
- CPU general managers with skills, traits and a franchise plan; their trades come from what they want and
  explain why they happened.
- Contracts: extensions, player and team options, stars who walk away from losing teams, same-night
  free-agency auctions.
- Draft: lottery, scouting, draft board, CPU picks by fit and GM skill, draft-night trades.
- Owners judge coaches and GMs; firings, hirings and retirements across the league.
- Day-by-day live simulation with Stop, or sim to the trade deadline, the All-Star break or the end of the
  regular season.
- Player database with full careers, League Analytics (advanced, rebounding, clutch, shot zones), awards,
  records, Hall of Fame and news.
- Saves are written all-or-nothing with a backup copy.
